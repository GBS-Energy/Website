PV + Storage: more benefit from every kWh

Excess solar power can be stored and used later or used to benefit the grid. The storage documents include, among other things: an integrated one EMS as well as cloud monitoring over the life cycle.

FAQ

Shortly answered – based on the “mega, watt that’s how it works”‑FAQ from the documents.

What happens when the sun doesn't shine?

Electricity is produced even on cloudy days – just less. If it is not enough, electricity from the public grid is automatically supplemented.

What are the advantages of solar power?

Solar power is cheap, emission-free, more independent and protects against sudden price jumps (e.g. due to crises or inflation).

Can unused electricity be stored?

Yes: Excess solar power can be temporarily stored in the battery storage and used later.

How does solar energy affect the environment?

Solar power reduced CO₂Emissions are significant because it is produced without fossil fuels.
