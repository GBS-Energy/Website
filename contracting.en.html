What contracting in multi-family homes actually means

We install and operate the energy infrastructure at our own expense. The electricity is used directly in the building and is intelligently backed up via storage plus residual electricity.

  • GBS Energy GmbH installs the PV-Equipment on the rental house at your own expense.
  • If necessary, we also integrate heat pumps, wall boxes and suitable measurement infrastructure.
  • The electricity generated on site is used directly in the building and fed into a large storage facility.
  • If local generation is not sufficient, additional electricity is purchased from a third party.
  • This creates a resilient supply solution with economic benefits for tenants and owners.
