What we stand for

As a company, we focus on clear, fair cooperation with owners, investors and partners. The focus is on reliability, transparency and economically sensible, long-term solutions in the energy transition.

  • Partnership at eye level

    We work together in a binding, open manner and with clear responsibilities in all project phases.

  • Transparency in decisions

    Decision-making processes, assumptions and next steps are communicated in a comprehensible and understandable manner.

  • Economically viable models

    We combine sustainability with resilient economic models for investors and owners.

  • Long term perspective

    Our focus is on stable partnerships and projects that create lasting added value.

Board

Strategy and implementation – responsible for operational management.

Robert Benecke

Board

Robert Benecke has more than 20 years of sales experience in products that require explanation in the B2B and B2C environment. His focus is on setting up and managing sales organizations, recruiting and developing training and mentoring concepts. He has been working in the renewable energy sector since 2022, with a focus on photovoltaics, heat pumps and battery storage projects. At GBS Energy GmbH he is responsible for sales development, structuring central sales processes and the scalable implementation of new market approaches.

Director of Sales

Since 2014, he has led several companies as managing director and brings his experience to the expansion of resilient sales structures. At GBS Energy GmbH he is responsible for the sales area and supports the operational development of market development.

Christian Potthoff

Chairman of the Supervisory Board

Christian Potthoff is a partner and managing director of the inter pares group of companies and has been active as an entrepreneur in the care industry since 1977. Jan-Christian Koch

Aufsichtsrat

Jan-Christian Koch supports the Supervisory Board in fundamental questions of corporate development and brings in perspectives for resilient, long-term viable decisions. It accompanies the strategic classification of key topics and promotes a balanced assessment of opportunities, risks and operational requirements. Carsten Schmidt

Aufsichtsrat

Since 2001, Carsten Schmidt has been admitted to the bar at the Bernau District Court near Berlin and at the Brandenburg Higher Regional Court. From 2001 to 2017 he was a partner in the law firm Dr. Schmidt Rechtsanwälte GbR, later fkfs Rechtsanwälte GbR. Silvano D'Agostino

Aufsichtsrat

Silvano D’Agostino is a native of Bremen and holds a Bachelor of Arts from Harvard University and a Master of Science from the London School of Economics in organizational psychology. For eight years he was a partner in a management consultancy with a focus on growth in medium-sized companies.
