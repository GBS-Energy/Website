What makes an area suitable?

We check transparently and quickly. Typical criteria include: Access, floor space and proximity to the grid connection (e.g. transformer/transfer point).

  • footprint

    A small, flat area of ​​land (e.g. around 4 m²) may be sufficient - depending on the project.

  • Access & Operations

    access for construction and maintenance; minor impairment in normal operation.

  • Netzanbindung

    The decisive factors are the proximity to the grid connection point/transformer and the technical feasibility.

  • Sicherheit

    Sound insulation and safety concepts are planned and implemented on a project-specific basis.

FAQ

Short answers to frequently asked questions.

Do I have to invest or take care of operations?

No. We take care of planning, construction, operation and maintenance – you just provide the space.

How does the exam work?

We start with a quick check and then check technical criteria such as network connection and access.

What happens to my area during operation?

The area is used for the system. Maintenance access is regulated and the effort required for you in everyday life is minimal.

Can I read the lease details?
