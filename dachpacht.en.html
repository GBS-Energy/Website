What roof lease models are there?

Depending on the roof, usage and consumption, we develop the right model. The selection is flexible and can be combined.

Classic roof lease

Fixed rental payment, the electricity generated is fed into the grid. You receive predictable income.

Roof lease with own electricity

You use part of the solar power yourself and reduce your energy costs.

Combo with storage & EMS

Additional flexibility through storage and energy management – ​​useful with suitable load profiles.

Requirements for roof lease

We check all technical and legal criteria. Roof quality, statics and network connection are crucial.

  • Roof area & statics

    Sufficient size, load capacity and reasonable remaining useful life.

  • Alignment & shading

    Good sun availability, as little shading as possible.

  • Netzanbindung

    Technical feasibility and suitable connection nearby.

  • Access and security

    Access for construction/maintenance as well as fire protection and security concept.

  • Legal clarifications

    Ownership, permits and clear contract terms.

FAQ

Short answers to frequently asked questions.

Do I have to invest or take care of the operation?

No. We finance, install, operate and insure the system - you provide the roof space.

What terms are usual?

The contracts are long-term. The specific term is agreed depending on the object and project.

Can I use solar power myself?

Yes, depending on the model and consumption profile. We check whether our own electricity makes economic sense.

What requirements does the roof have to meet?

Statics, roof condition, alignment, shading and network connection are checked.

What happens at the end of the contract?

Typical options are extension, takeover or dismantling. The contract regulates the details.

Are the income taxable?

Rental income may be taxable. Please clarify details with your tax advisor.
