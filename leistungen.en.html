Battery storage projects

Battery storage combines generation and consumption, reacts in seconds and can be operated to benefit the grid. In the technical documentation (CESC Mercury) are u. a. integrated EMS, lifecycle cloud monitoring as well as remote upgrades and remote control.

  • Project development & implementation

    Site inspection, grid connection, approvals, construction, commissioning – structured and comprehensible.

  • Technical system design

    Components such as battery pack, power electronics (PCS), distribution as well as cooling system and fire protection as a complete system.

  • Security & protection concept

    Thermal management, fire/smoke detection and protection levels – for predictable operation and minimized risk.

  • Network-friendly operation

    Operating modes for load shifting, peak shaving and rapid response to grid demands.

Energy management (EMS) & Monitoring

Laut EMSPlatform specification includes central functions, among others: Plant overview, real-time data, centralized control, alarms/warnings, Reports and upgrade management. It is precisely this transparency that is crucial for reliable operations.

Real-time transparency

Status, SoC, device communication, measured values ​​– clear and comprehensible.

Alarm & Reports

Warnings, events, evaluations (e.g. monthly report) and clear response paths.

Zentralsteuerung

Control of components such as PCS, meters, thermal management and other systems.

Updates & remote access

Upgrade management and remote control/parameters – with clean documentation.

Technical documents (PDF): EMS Plattform · Mercury product specification

Operation, maintenance & support

The product specification includes, among other things: Maintenance chapters and safety systems (cooling, fire protection) are described. What matters most for owners and operators is clear responsibility, predictable availability and short response times.

  • Monitoring & availability

    Monitoring system status, thermal management and events – with clear escalation paths.

  • Dokumentation

    Measurement, maintenance and event logs as a basis for transparency and acceptance.

  • Sicherheitsbetrieb

    Protective functions, detection and fire protection systems – taken into account during operation.

  • Remote access & updates

    Update processes and remote control for quick optimizations and troubleshooting.
