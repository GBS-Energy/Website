Requirements for leasing open space

We check all technical and legal criteria. The decisive factors are the size of the area, development, proximity to the grid and permits.

  • Area size & cutting

    Sufficient area, sensible geometry and suitable use.

  • Development & infrastructure

    Access, cable routes and existing infrastructure nearby.

  • Netzanbindung

    Technical feasibility and suitable connection at the location.

  • Soil & Nature Conservation

    Soil reports, environmental and nature conservation requirements are checked.

  • Legal clarifications

    Ownership, permits and clear contract terms.

Differentiation from roof leasing & contracting

Area leasing of open land is an area leasing on land. Other models focus more on building use or the power supply for users.

Roof Lease

Leasing of roof areas on buildings – focus on building areas instead of open land.

Contracting

The operator supplies electricity to users. Focus: Energy supply instead of land leasing.

FAQ

Short answers to frequently asked questions.

Which areas are considered privileged?

Privileged areas are locations that are particularly suitable due to the existing infrastructure and location.

Do I have to invest or take care of the operation?

No. We finance, install, operate and insure the system - you provide the property.

What terms are usual?

The contracts are long-term. The specific duration is agreed depending on the location and project.

How does the approval work?

We take care of the approval and coordination processes and keep you up to date.

What happens at the end of the contract?

Typical options are extension, takeover or dismantling. The contract regulates the details.

Are the income taxable?

Rental income may be taxable. Please clarify details with your tax advisor.
