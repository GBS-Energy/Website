Photovoltaics at a glance

Photovoltaics deliver cheap, emission-free electricity. We support you in designing and selling the right system – and combine photovoltaics with battery storage and energy management systems if necessary.

What we deliver

From the initial assessment to commissioning: we structure the process and ensure clear, comprehensible steps.

Advice & preliminary examination

Area, orientation, shading, load profile and initial yield estimate.

Interpretation & offer

Component selection, dimensioning and clear offer and scope of delivery.

Implementation with partners

Coordination of installation and commissioning – with clean acceptance.

Optional: Monitoring

If desired, with monitoring and evaluation logic – particularly useful in combination with storage.

Photovoltaics + storage: more benefits every kilowatt hour

Excess solar power can be stored and used later or used to benefit the grid. The system includes, among other things, an integrated energy management system and cloud monitoring across the full life cycle.

More self-consumption

Electricity is available at different times - for example in the evening or at night.

More stable network

Storage can smooth out load peaks and react flexibly.

More transparency

Energy management systems and monitoring help to make the status and performance understandable.

Scalable

Modular systems – suitable for the application and location.
